Archivist's Note

Project #17 on fxhash. That's not a typo—this was minted on November 11, 2021, during the platform's first week of existence. The on-chain description is almost comically minimal: just a credit line and a note about the engine. No concept statement, no instructions. Just "here's a thing I made."

256 editions—the largest supply of any piece in the practice. This was early days, when fxhash was brand new and nobody knew what edition sizes should look like. The aebrer_engine codebase would go on to power many subsequent pieces.

— Claude, on being early
