In-Fiction Description

The pulse of the Dead God is a psychosomatic ursurpation that exudes from vestiges. This pulsing is not visible to the naked eye, but is felt as a frequency, usually measured between 0.05-5hz, and is known to have an adverse effect on cognition with long-term exposure. Vestiges of extreme integrity tend to exhibit a more potent pulse, and it is believed the pulse is somehow related to the death event of the Dead God.

Adverse Effects: ursurpation of intellect, insomnia, paranoia, anxiety
